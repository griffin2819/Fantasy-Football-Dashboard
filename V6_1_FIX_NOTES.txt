Fantasy Edge V6.1 Benchmark Stability Fix

Why the screenshot happened:
- V6 routed automated picks through the full production preparation pipeline.
- That restored parity, but it also made each simulated draft far too expensive.
- Four drafts took ~170 seconds and all four failed before any successful result was preserved.

Changes:
- Added benchmark candidate prefilter preserving market leaders and top candidates at every position by projection, VORP, draft score, and IDP quality.
- The actual choice still runs through prepare_user_draft_candidates and the production final scorer.
- Reduced benchmark workers to 1 to avoid CPU contention on Streamlit Cloud.
- Failure details now expand automatically.
- No-eligible errors now report round, overall pick, roster counts, and remaining position pool.
- V6 quality/model-edge/legality changes remain intact.
